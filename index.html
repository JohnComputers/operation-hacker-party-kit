Mission overview

One story. Six satisfying breakthroughs.

The site runs the digital trail. The printable evidence gets agents moving, collaborating, and looking closely.

The 20-minute runbook

Prep the evidence, then get out of the way.

  1. 01

    Print and fold

    Cut each active receipt. Fold the barcode panel behind the receipt face so the digits start hidden.

  2. 02

    Pack the envelopes

    Cut each five-letter label into strips, shuffle the pieces with its dispatch note, and mark the envelope with the team name and DAMAGED LABEL.

  3. 03

    Hide the evidence

    Place Hawk under a chair, Wolf behind a planter, and Bolt under a mat. Keep the prize table out of sight.

  4. 04

    Stage the archive

    Use the built-in images, or photograph each place with its parcel ID sign and a visible envelope corner. Remove the signs afterward.

  5. 05

    Test and reset

    Run every active route through ACCESS GRANTED, restart each device, and place the matching folded receipt face down.

One hint for each sticking pointKeep the race moving without giving answers away
  • Start: Which number says tracking?
  • Portal: What would a driver click after forgetting a PIN?
  • Digits: Touch the arrow. Which digit is closest?
  • Photo: Match every character in the parcel ID.
  • Label: Find the outside edges and connect the diagonal line.
Safety and fair playA quick adult checklist
  • Keep the search area small, well lit, and free of traffic or trip hazards.
  • Use only fictional tracking numbers and never enter real delivery information.
  • Tell agents to open only their labeled envelope and leave other property alone.
  • Ghost is fictional. This game teaches observation and teamwork, not real system access.
